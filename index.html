FROM AFAR: It is most convenient to arrive at Newark Airport (EWR) in New Jersey. It is also possible to arrive at any NYC (JFK, LGA) or Philadelphia area (PHL) airports.

FROM NYC OR NEWARK AIRPORT: Train service via NJ Transit operates from Penn Station New York or from Newark Airport Station. From either origin, take the Northeast Corridor line trains toward Trenton and get off at the Hamilton train station. Tickets can be purchased on the NJ Transit phone app. From there, you may take an Uber to the hotel or venue.

FROM NEARBY: The Ashford Estate is a short drive off the NJ Turnpike Exit 8a.



STAY OVERNIGHT: A discounted room block has been reserved at the Homewood Suites by Hilton in Hamilton, New Jersey. You are welcome to stay at the hotel whether you are joining us from afar or locally! Shuttle service will be provided between the hotel and the venue.

To book a room for the night of the wedding, please use this link. Note that some options are suites with more than one bedroom - these might be good for groups traveling together. Rooms MUST be reserved by June 15th, 2026 - book in advance to ensure you get a spot!







Q: IS THERE A DRESS CODE?
A: The dress code for our wedding is formal. Evening gowns, long or mid-length dresses, tuxedos, or a dark suit and tie would all be appropriate!


Q: IS THERE A GIFT REGISTRY?
A: We don't have a traditional registry. If you'd like to give a gift, we've set up a honeymoon fund. A link to contribute via Venmo is below—but most of all, we're simply excited to celebrate with you! contribute honeymoon fund (via Venmo)


Q: WHICH EVENTS ARE INDOORS? OUTDOORS?
A: Weather permitting, our ceremony will be outdoors. All other events will take place indoors. As the wedding is in August, expect hot temperatures.


Q: ARE ALL EVENTS AT THE SAME LOCATION?
A: All events are at different sites within the Ashford Estate, all within walking distance of one another. Transportation between events will not be required.


Q: HOW DO I GET TO/FROM THE VENUE?
A: If you are staying in the hotel room block, shuttle service will be provided to and from the venue. There will be one arrival pick-up before the event at 4:30pm. Departure pick-ups will be scheduled for immediately after the reception (11pm), during the after-party (12am), and at the end of the after-party (1am). Ubers are an alternative option to get to/from the event without a car. If you are planning to drive, there is ample parking at the venue.


Q: IS THERE PARKING?
A: Yes! There is ample parking at the venue.


Q: CAN I BRING CHILDREN AND/OR A PLUS ONE?
A: All invited guests, including plus ones and children, are explicitly named on your invitation and in the RSVP form. Please let us know if you have any questions.


Q: WHAT IF I HAVE DIETARY RESTRICTIONS?
A: Please let Cassandra or Doron know and we will ensure our caterer can accommodate you.
